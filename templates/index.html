Why we're honest about this

Most "AI visibility" tools sell you an llms.txt file as if it were magic. The 2026 server-log evidence says otherwise: ~97% of llms.txt files receive zero requests, and no major AI provider confirms consuming it. So we weight it 5 points out of 100 — cheap hygiene, not a lever.

What actually gets you customers from agents is unglamorous: structured business data plus a booking or ordering flow the agent can complete. That's what 45 of our 100 points measure.
